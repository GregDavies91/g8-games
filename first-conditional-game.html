ESL First Conditional Game

English G8-1 · Grammar and Speaking · Forming and Answering Questions from Prompts
Upper-intermediate (B2) ⏱ 25 minutes
In this first conditional game, students practise asking first conditional questions and responding with phrases of probability. Write probability phrases on the board (I doubt if…, It's unlikely…, will definitely, will probably, etc.). In pairs, students take turns picking up an if-clause card and asking the student on their right a first conditional question using the clause. If they pick up a blank If… card, they create their own first conditional question. If the question is grammatically correct and logical, the student scores a point. The other student then answers using a phrase of probability from the board. If the answer is grammatically correct, the answering student also scores a point. The student with the most points wins.
