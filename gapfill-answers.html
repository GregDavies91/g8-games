🔑 Gap-Fill Game — Answer Key

English G8-1 · For teachers only · Do not distribute to students

📋 Scoring Rules

  • Students guess the missing words: have/has (auxiliary) and a verb (past participle).
  • One guess for have/has → correct = 1 point.
  • Two guesses for the verb → correct on first or second try = 1 point.
  • If the verb is wrong after two guesses, the partner reveals the answer → 0 points.
  • After revealing both questions, the guessing student asks the two questions to their partner (speaking practice).
  • Student with the most points at the end wins.

💡 Teaching note: Students are checking each other's grammar — this is intentional. They practice saying "Have you..." or "has..." and their partner confirms or corrects. This is controlled practice (form-focused) that leads into freer speaking (asking and answering the follow-up questions). If a student is unsure about the answer, encourage them to ask you rather than guessing randomly — this keeps the grammar focus sharp.
