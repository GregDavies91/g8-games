📋 How to Play

  • Roll a dice and move along the path.
  • Answer the prompt out loud using present perfect.
  • Correct → stay. Wrong → go back 2 spaces.
  • Follow special spaces (skip / miss).
  • First to FINISH wins!

✅ Grammar Reminders

  • have/has + past participle
  • ever/never in questions
  • for/since with time
  • just/already/yet/recently
  • "I have gone" not "I have went"

📦 What You Need

  • 1 dice (or phone app)
  • 1 counter per player
  • 2–4 players (or teams)
  • Optional: notebook for sentences
