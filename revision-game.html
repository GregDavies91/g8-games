Before the exam, make sure you can…

  • Use past simple for finished actions: We went to the beach.
  • Choose present simple (habits) vs present continuous (now): I play chess / I am playing now.
  • Use going to (plans) vs will (decisions): I'm going to visit / I'll help you.
  • Use used to for past habits: He used to go every day.
  • Know your holiday words: boat trip, souvenir, old town, hire, theatre, theme park, market.
