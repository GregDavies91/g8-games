English G8-1 · Present Perfect Simple vs Continuous · Unit 2D

Your task: Read both passages carefully. Find at least 3 present perfect sentences. Circle them. Then write S (Simple) or C (Continuous) and why.
Key clue: Simple = the result matters (finished). Continuous = the activity matters (still going).

Passage A — A Letter Home

Dear Mum and Dad,
I have been having a wonderful time at the summer camp.
The days are long but full of activities.
I have tried archery, climbing and canoeing.
My favourite so far is photography — I have been taking pictures of everything.
Remember the camera you gave me for my birthday?
My dad has had a camera for as long as I can remember, but I have never had a really good camera of my own.
Now I do!
I have been doing photography at a local club since September and I have learned so much.
I have already taken about 200 photos with it.
I have been working hard on my schoolwork lately too.
The teachers here make everything interesting.
I hope you are well and I will write again soon.
Love, Alex

Passage B — A Magazine Interview

Today we talk to two talented siblings about their hobbies.
I have tried lots of different activities over the years, but basketball is my passion.
I have been playing basketball for three years now and I love every minute.
I have also been going to a gymnastics club since I was eight — it helps with my flexibility.
My sister is different.
She has never played a team sport.
She prefers individual activities.
We both have been spending a lot of time on our hobbies lately.
She has been going skating since she was little and she is very good now.
Thank you for reading!
