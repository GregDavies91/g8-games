🎯 Present Perfect Gap-Fill Game

English G8-1 · Pair Work · Controlled → Freer Practice

📋 How to Play

  1. Work in pairs. One student is Student A, the other is Student B.
  2. Take turns guessing the missing words in each question.
  3. The missing words are: have/has (auxiliary) and a verb (past participle).
  4. You get one guess for have/has and two guesses for the verb.
  5. Each correct answer = 1 point. If you guess the verb wrong twice, your partner tells you the answer.
  6. After revealing both questions, the guessing student asks the two questions to their partner.
  7. The student with the most points at the end wins!
